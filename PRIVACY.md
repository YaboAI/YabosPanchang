# Privacy Policy — Panchāng

_Last updated: [date]_

Panchāng — Daily Hindu Almanac ("the app") does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **No location access.** The city you pick is chosen manually from a list.
- **Stored on your device only:** your settings (for example theme and selected city) are kept in the app's local storage and never leave your device.
- **Fonts:** the app loads two typefaces (Fraunces and Work Sans) from Google Fonts. When it does, Google's servers receive your device's IP address, as with any web request. See Google's Fonts privacy information for details.
- **Children:** the app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.
