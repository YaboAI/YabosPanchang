**App name (30 chars):** Panchāng – Hindu Almanac

**Short description (80 chars):**
Daily Panchang, tithi, festivals, Ekadashi & vrats, Navratri details and muhurat.

**Full description:**
Panchāng is a clean daily Hindu almanac that works for your city.

• Daily panchang: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, sunset, Rahu Kaal and more
• Festivals 2020–2036 with a story and details card for each one
• Ekadashi, Pradosh, Sankashti, Vinayaka Chaturthi, Purnima and Amavasya marked in the calendar
• Navratri details: dates, Devi for each day, planet and colour, Ghatasthapana and Sandhi Puja timings, puja vidhi
• Monthly calendar with festivals and vrats
• Vedic clock (ghati, pal, vipal) and prahar classification in the Yantrāṇi tab
• Swipe between days, months and tabs; light, saffron and midnight themes
• Works offline. No account, no ads.

Dates are calculated and can differ by a day from a local panchang near tithi boundaries. Please confirm important muhurats with your family priest.
