Privacy Policy

Panchāng — Daily Hindu Almanac · Last updated 21 October 2026

In short: Panchāng does not have user accounts and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Everything it calculates runs on your own device.

Who we are

Panchāng (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a Hindu almanac app that shows the daily panchang, festivals, muhurat, and astrology tools. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app has no login, no sign-up, and no analytics or advertising trackers.

Information stored only on your device

Some information is saved locally on your device so the app can remember your choices. It stays on your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. This includes:

  • your selected city or location, and app settings such as the theme and reminder preference;
  • any birth details you enter for the Kundali, Dasha, Gochar, or Kundali Match tools (name, date, time, and place of birth), and any Kundalis or matches you choose to save.

Location

The app does not request your device’s GPS location. You choose a city from a list, or enter coordinates yourself, and that choice is used only to calculate the panchang. It is stored only on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules a local notification on your device for the evening before a festival or vrat. These reminders are created and delivered entirely on your device. No reminder data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app’s settings at any time, or disable notifications for the app in your device settings.

Internet and fonts

The app is designed to work offline. When you are online, it may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can expose your device’s IP address to Google under Google’s privacy policy. The app itself does not use this for any tracking.

Children

The app is a general-audience almanac and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Because the app does not collect your data, there is nothing for us to share.

Your control

You can delete everything the app has stored by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Saved Kundalis and matches can also be deleted individually inside the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us through the app’s Google Play listing.

This policy applies to the Panchāng Android app and the Panchāng website.
